# Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before Day One

Once your signed contract is returned, IT ships your laptop and provisions your accounts at least 2 business days before your start date. You will receive an email with your first-day schedule and the contact details of your onboarding buddy.

## First Day

Meet your manager at 09:30, collect your building badge from reception, and complete laptop setup with IT. You will enroll in multi-factor authentication and install the company password manager and VPN during this session.

## First Week

Complete the mandatory courses within your first 5 business days: Security Awareness, Code of Conduct and Data Privacy. Your onboarding buddy will introduce you to your team and walk you through the tools you will use. Your manager will agree your 30-60-90 day goals with you.

## Benefits Enrollment

You must enroll in benefits within 30 days of your start date through the HR portal. Available benefits are medical, dental and vision insurance, and the retirement savings plan, where the company matches contributions up to 4%. If you do not enroll within 30 days you must wait for the next open enrollment period unless you have a qualifying life event.

## Probation and Reviews

The probation period is 90 days. Your manager holds check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days, and the 90-day review confirms your employment and, where applicable, your eligibility for hybrid work.

## Who To Ask

For leave and benefits questions, contact HR through the HR portal. For equipment, accounts and access, contact the IT Service Desk. For expense questions, contact Finance.
