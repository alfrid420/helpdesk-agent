# Expense Policy

## General Principles

Expenses must be business-related, reasonable and supported by documentation. Submit claims within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted later than 60 days require Finance approval and may be declined.

## Meals and Entertainment

The limit for a client dinner is $75 per person, including tax and tip. Team meals are limited to $35 per person. Alcohol is reimbursable only at client dinners and counts toward the $75 limit. Always list the names of all attendees and the business purpose on the claim.

## Receipts

An itemized receipt is required for every expense above $25. A card slip showing only the total is not accepted. For expenses of $25 or less, a short note describing the business purpose is enough. Lost receipts require a signed missing-receipt form approved by your manager.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the company travel portal. Economy class applies to flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Hotels are limited to $220 per night in major cities and $160 per night elsewhere. Daily meal allowance while travelling is $60. Use public transport or standard taxis rather than premium car services.

## Approvals

Managers approve expenses up to $1,000. The Finance Manager approves expenses above $1,000. Any single purchase above $5,000 needs written approval before you commit to it. You cannot approve your own expenses.

## Reimbursement Timing

Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 business days of approval, in the next regular payment run. Rejected claims are returned with a reason so you can resubmit.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

Personal items, fines and penalties, commuting costs, gifts to colleagues above $50, and expenses for family members are not reimbursable.
