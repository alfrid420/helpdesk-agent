# Information Security Policy

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and unique to each system. A company-approved password manager is mandatory. Passwords for standard accounts must be rotated every 180 days. Passwords for privileged and administrator accounts must be rotated every 90 days. Reusing any of your last 10 passwords is not permitted. Passwords must be changed immediately if compromise is suspected.

Never share a password with anyone, including IT staff. IT will never ask for your password.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required on all company systems. Administrators must use hardware security keys. SMS codes are allowed only as a fallback for standard accounts.

## Devices

All company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system and security updates must be installed within 14 days of release. Personal USB storage devices must not be connected to company equipment.

## Data Classification

Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data must be stored only in approved company systems and must not be sent to personal email accounts or unapproved AI tools.

## Reporting Incidents

Report any suspected phishing, malware, lost or stolen device, or data exposure within 1 hour of noticing it. Use the #security-incidents channel or the IT Service Desk portal. Do not try to investigate or delete evidence yourself. Reporting in good faith will never be penalized.

## Acceptable Use

Company systems are for business use, with limited reasonable personal use. Do not install unapproved software, bypass security controls, or access data you do not need for your job.
