# Leave Policy

This policy applies to all Acme employees. "Full-time" means a contracted 35 or more hours per week.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited on January 1. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on contracted hours. Employees who join part-way through the year receive a pro-rated entitlement for the remaining months. Employees at Director level and above receive 25 days.

## Carry-Over

Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31, otherwise they expire. Any unused days above the 5-day limit are forfeited on December 31. Annual leave is not paid out in cash except on termination of employment, where accrued untaken days are settled in the final payment.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Notify your manager before the start of your working day. A doctor's certificate is required after 3 consecutive sick days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Personal Days

Employees receive 3 paid personal days per calendar year for any reason, with no explanation required. Give at least 2 business days' notice where possible. Personal days do not carry over.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave and secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement of the child. Notify HR at least 30 days before the intended start date where possible.

## Requesting Leave

Submit all requests through the HR portal. Requests of 5 or more consecutive days should be submitted at least 10 business days in advance. Shorter requests need at least 2 business days' notice. Your manager will approve or decline within 3 business days. Approved leave appears in your leave history.
