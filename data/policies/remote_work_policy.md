# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees are eligible for hybrid work after successfully completing the 90-day probation period. Roles that require on-site presence, such as facilities and the IT service desk, may be excluded. Your manager confirms eligibility in writing.

## Hybrid Schedule

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are core in-office days for all hybrid teams, and attendance on those days is expected unless your manager agrees otherwise. Teams may agree a different pattern in writing if business needs require it.

## Working From Another Location

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year, subject to approval from both their manager and HR. Submit the request at least 30 days in advance, because tax, immigration and insurance rules may apply. Working abroad without approval is not permitted.

## Home Office Support

New remote-eligible employees receive a one-time $400 home office stipend for a desk, chair or similar equipment. The company also reimburses internet costs up to $50 per month with a monthly expense claim. Equipment bought with the stipend remains company property if you leave within 12 months.

## Availability

Remote employees must be reachable during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone, and must keep calendars current. Meetings should be scheduled within core hours wherever possible.

## Security When Working Remotely

Use the company VPN on any public or shared network. Do not use shared or family computers for company work. Lock your screen when stepping away. See the Information Security Policy for the full requirements.
